# Decision log

Translated and condensed from section 2 of the private working journal ("decisions made — do not
reopen without a reason"). Each row records the decision, the evidence it was based on and, where
it matters, the bias it was made under. Dates are 2026.

| Date | Decision | Evidence / rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 10 Aug | **Rewrite the core instead of repairing the legacy UI.** | Wrong math in every layer (float precision, incomplete liquidity formula, mixed units) masked by a hard-coded 20–25 % slippage tolerance. Cheaper to write cleanly. |
| 10 Aug | **No own smart contract / vault.** Positions stay as NFTs in the owner's wallet. | $5–25k of capital does not justify contract risk and audit cost. |
| 10 Aug | **Backtesting before any deployment.** Gate: a strategy must beat HODL 50/50 in ≥ 2 market regimes. | Defined before the strategies so it cannot be tuned to them. |
| 10 Aug | **TypeScript monorepo: core / data / backtest / bot / ui.** | One math module shared by every layer. |
| 10 Aug | **SQLite + CSV ledger from the first transaction.** | Tax reporting and auditability. |
| 11 Aug | **ALGORITHM v1 frozen**: k = 3 (ETH/stable), k = 2 (cbBTC), hysteresis 24 h, payback ≤ 7 d, trend breaker = plain exit on a 5 % break below the 7-day EMA. | 365-day walk-forward plus 5 out-of-sample cross-validation runs; the exit profile was the best outside the tuning pool and had the fewest parameters. |
| 11 Aug | **Revision v1.1**: re-entry after a drop = price back above EMA (ETH/stable); cbBTC keeps plain exit. | Full 365-day runs on two pools (22 windows each) reversed the morning ranking, which had been based on 4-window 90-day runs. First full gate pass on base-005 (68 % win rate, worst window −2.52 %). |
| 17 Aug | **Revision v1.2**: on base-030 the breaker becomes *hedge-excess* (short the ETH above 50 % on a perp, keep the LP); until a perp venue is integrated, exit remains the fallback. | The only configuration that closed the gate on base-030 on both windows (73 %/−2.88, 81 %/−2.74); historical funding +2.9 %/yr for the short. Full hedge and hedging on mainnet/005 rejected. |
| 18 Aug | **Auto-pull runner on Windows withdrawn**; code reaches the server only by a manual `git pull`; the only automated git action is the morning-report push. | Queue unused for a week; a `reset --hard` every 3 minutes is the risk class behind an incident on 17 Aug. Fewer moving parts. |
| 19 Aug | **`public/bundle.js` untracked.** | A tracked build artefact blocked the morning-report push after every build (three failures in one day). |
| 19 Aug | **After every pipeline fix, verify manually the same day** on the server; the 07:30 automation is routine, not the only test. | Three consecutive days the pipeline failed on a different cause; one debug cycle per day was too slow. |
| 19 Aug | **Pin `@envio-dev/hypersync-client` to exactly 1.0.0 repo-wide.** | Envio ships no win32 binary after 1.0.0; the caret range resolved to a wrapper without the native module on Windows while working on macOS. The API used is identical in 1.0.0. |
| 26 Aug | **Weekly review: σ on a 15-minute grid + full recalibration of k** on 365 d and 720 d. | The swap-based estimator measured pool microstructure, not the asset (σ spread 4.5× → 1.4× on the same ETH). Every k had been calibrated in broken units; the fix and the recalibration cannot be separated. |
| 26 Aug | **Validation gate = 720-day window + a recent ~90-day condition** (instead of 365 d). | The 365-day year (sideways-to-down) flattered the strategy; on 720 d **0 of 21 configurations** passed. The recent condition prevents old turbulence from disqualifying a pool for years. |
| 26 Aug | **Capital waits in USDC** until recalibration results; no Aave parking. Revised the same day to a *conditional entry*: enter if a pool passes the 720 d + recent-90 gate on the recalibrated runs. | Entering a narrow LP on a calibration we had just declared broken made no sense; small stake, sideways market, value of the operational experiment. Recorded with a FOMO note. |
| 26 Aug | **Operating hours: signatures 09:00–20:00 Mon–Fri; the trend-breaker alarm runs 24/7.** Measure the cost of signature delay from day one. | Propose-only mode executes nothing itself; delay is the only risk (night ≈ 13 h, weekend ≈ 61 h). |
| 27 Aug | **Product = FlatWide hybrid on both pools, whole tranche (6 092 USDC, Base).** Wide passive ranges (±50 % / ±40 %); narrow (k×σ) only in a confirmed flat (\|gap\| < 2 % for 12 h). | Tested before entry on 365 d × 2 pools: hybrid ≥ pure passive on both; on cbBTC the closest any strategy came to passing the gate. Entered after a +25 %/90 d ETH move — recorded as a reverse-FOMO note. |
| 31 Aug | **Sign the first narrowing proposal (cbBTC leg) as an operational experiment**, n = 1, explicitly *not* a test of the strategy; the 720-day falsification stands. | We wanted real `widthPct` / `costUsd` / `paybackDays` and a full narrow→wide cycle measured live. |
| 2 Sep | **Tranche exit rule**: exit the LP on 24 Sep regardless of price; earlier if the tranche balance reaches +5 %. Manual daily monitoring, deliberately no automation. | The product beats HODL in none of five measurements (13-pool scan, wide score, Monte Carlo, daily model, full replay); HODL + ~4 %/yr yield wins without effort. Set while calm, not on a big-move day. |
| 11 Sep | **Close the narrowing episode** (trend breaker fired on cbBTC): sign the widening; reject both emergency options (short on GMX, exit to cash). Product unchanged. | Signal only on the crypto/crypto leg; on such a pair LP and HODL share the same USD beta. Episode: 11 days in flat, ≈ $8.6 fees, −$32 vs HODL. |
| 16 Sep | **Exit rule revised** (day after a −4 % move): exit the LP when the tranche balance ≥ deposit (0 %); the hard 24 Sep exit and the +5 % trigger are dropped; 24 Sep becomes a review; a balance ≤ −10 % forces a new decision; bet review on 24 Oct; still no narrowing. | Loss was entirely market beta (vs HODL −$12); the owner kept a conscious bet on an ETH/BTC recovery. Recorded explicitly as a rule change made the day after a drop. |
| 18 Sep | **LP unwound at break-even** (+0.6 %, +$38, 22 days) when the rule triggered. **Tokens kept as a spot bet** (~70 % of the tranche in ETH/cbBTC), floor at −5 %, default sale on 24 Oct; USDC moved to the exchange. | Wide LP added nothing to the directional bet (vs HODL −$4…−$22 over 3 weeks). Recorded as the second rule change in one week, both in the "hold" direction. |
| 21 Sep | **Everything sold to EUR** during a short-squeeze rally (BTC at the $85–86k ETF cost-basis / liquidation cluster, ETH at the $2 750–2 800 resistance analysts had named that morning). Tranche closed at **+5.5 %** (+$336) in 25 days. | Discretionary sale "into strength" announced in the morning and executed after the US open as promised. Recorded as a decision, not a rule: the method "sell at the top" is not repeatable and must not be treated as validated by the outcome. |
| 22 Sep | **Project closed.** Repository converted into a public case study; private journal retained in history and in a private clone. | The question was answered; further capital would be a bet on beta, not on the product. |
