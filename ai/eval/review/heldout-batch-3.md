## ho-id-03 (identifier, answerable: yes, by CC-Mac)

**Question.** What does an UNMAPPED verdict mean in the candidate funnel?

**Reference.** The funnel validates pools from the DefiLlama ranking, and first maps each one to its on-chain address through a dictionary of known major tokens per network, factory.getPool and a sanity check of token0/token1. When a token is outside the dictionary or the sanity check disagrees, the verdict is UNMAPPED: the pool goes to manual review and the funnel never guesses an address or runs on silently wrong data. Exotic pairs without an ETH/BTC or stable leg are UNMAPPED by rule. Eligibility and OPEN proposals skip FAIL and UNMAPPED pools. In the first full backfill (25.08) all 5 candidates were mapped, zero UNMAPPED.
